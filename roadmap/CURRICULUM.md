## Weekly evidence checkpoint

For every week retain:

1. A working release and clean setup instructions.
2. Automated checks and one reproduced failure.
3. Architecture/data-flow note and at least one decision record.
4. Accessibility, security and performance evidence proportional to scope.
5. A two-minute demo and a five-minute no-notes technical explanation.
6. Known limitations and exactly one next action.
