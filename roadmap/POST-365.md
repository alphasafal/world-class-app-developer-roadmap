# After Day 365: The Principal/CTO Continuation

## Years 2–3: production depth

- Own one product with real users for 12+ months.
- Rotate through reliability, security, data, native performance and release ownership.
- Lead a substantial migration and publish its decision record and measured outcome.
- Contribute meaningfully to React Native, Expo, Swift/Kotlin or a respected mobile library.
- Mentor developers and practice high-quality code/design reviews.

## Years 3–5: principal scope

- Set technical direction across several teams or comparable open-source/product surfaces.
- Build a platform that accelerates other engineers.
- Handle incidents, ambiguous tradeoffs and multi-quarter migrations.
- Demonstrate business impact, not only technical complexity.
- Write and defend strategy with cost, security, compliance and organizational constraints.

## Founder/CTO track

- Conduct 100+ customer conversations across multiple product cycles.
- Ship narrow paid products; learn pricing, support, privacy, contracts and unit economics.
- Build hiring scorecards, engineering principles, delivery cadence, threat model, SLOs and cost model.
- Know when not to write software and when to replace your own architecture.

Every quarter: repeat the rubric, request blunt external review, remove one vanity goal and choose one outcome that matters to users.
